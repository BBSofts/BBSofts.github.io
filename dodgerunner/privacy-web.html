Privacy Policy (Web Version)

Effective date: October 11, 2026 |  Last updated: October 11, 2026

BBFactory ("we," "us," or "our") provides the web game DodgeRunner (닷지 러너) (the "Game" or the "Service"). This Privacy Policy applies to the web version of the Game offered on web game platforms such as CrazyGames. It explains what information we collect while providing the Service, why we use it, and how we store, protect, and delete it. The Android app is covered by the Android app Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect and How We Collect It

The Game does not require sign-up. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment information.

CategoryInformation collectedHow it is collected
Guest account A randomly generated guest identifier created in your browser, or the user ID provided by the platform (see below), and a user ID issued by our server Created automatically the first time the Game is launched
Gameplay information Nickname, best score, league tier, rank and points, in-game coins, and unlocked maps Generated during gameplay and synchronized with our server
Access information Times at which the Game signs in to or saves data on our server Recorded automatically by our server

If you launch the Game while signed in to a CrazyGames account, the Game uses the user ID provided by CrazyGames instead of a random guest identifier, so that your game data follows your account. We do not store any other CrazyGames account information, such as your username, profile picture, or email address, on our server.

Your game progress and sign-in session are also stored in your browser's local storage.

Your nickname, score, and league rank are visible to other players on in-game leaderboards. Please do not use personal information such as your real name as your nickname.

2. Purposes of Collection and Use

  • To provide the game service, identify guest accounts, and save or synchronize game data
  • To operate leaderboards, leagues, in-game currency, and unlockable content
  • To prevent abuse, diagnose errors, respond to user inquiries, and improve service stability
  • To understand overall service usage, such as how many players return to the Game

3. Advertising and the Hosting Platform

In the web version, we do not use Google AdMob or any other advertising network of our own. Any ads shown in or around the Game are provided by the platform on which the Game is offered. That platform may collect information such as cookies and device information to operate its service and show ads, and its own privacy policy applies to that processing. For CrazyGames, see the CrazyGames Privacy Policy.

4. Retention and Use Period

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the Service and fulfill the purposes described in this Policy. If a user requests account deletion or data deletion, we delete the relevant information without undue delay. Information that must be retained under applicable laws may be stored separately for the required retention period and then deleted.

5. Sharing Personal Information with Third Parties

We do not sell or arbitrarily provide users' personal information to external parties without prior consent. Exceptions may apply when disclosure is required by law or when the user has directly consented.

7. User Rights and How to Exercise Them

Users may exercise the following rights at any time:

  • Request access to personal information
  • Request correction or deletion of personal information
  • Request suspension of personal information processing

You can change your nickname at any time in the Game's settings screen. For other requests, contact us at the email address below. Because the Game uses guest accounts, we identify your account by the nickname shown in the settings screen, so please include it in your request.

8. Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your guest account and all data stored on our server as follows. Deleted data cannot be recovered.

  1. Open the settings screen in the Game and check your nickname.
  2. Send an email to playdysoft@gmail.com with the subject "DodgeRunner data deletion request" and include your nickname in the message.

We will delete the data within 7 days of receiving your request and reply with the result. Without your nickname we cannot locate your account, which may delay deletion.

  • Information stored as electronic files is deleted securely using methods that make recovery difficult.
  • Clearing your browser's site data removes the game data stored in your browser, but does not delete the data on our server. Please submit a deletion request if you want it removed.

9. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 14. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will delete it.
